Noun Incorporation

When a noun (argument attached to the verb) gets incorporated into the verb (mostly becomes a single word).

Example

In Yucatec Mayan (spoken in Mexico and Belize; SVO)

Before incorporation, verb and D.O. are separate words:

tin’akahče’
t-in-č’ak-Ø’ahče’
COMP-1SG-chop-it-ASPtree

'I chopped a tree'
